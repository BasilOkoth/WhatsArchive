# ChatArchive Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 15 September 2026**

ChatArchive is a product of **Elimara Technologies Limited**. This policy explains how ChatArchive handles information when you use the Android application.

## 1. What ChatArchive does

ChatArchive creates a private local archive from supported messaging-app notifications. The app is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.

## 2. Notification Access

If you choose to enable Android Notification Access, ChatArchive may read notification information that Android makes available from supported messaging apps. This may include the conversation or sender name, message text, notification time, and attachment information or attachment content when Android exposes a readable attachment URI.

ChatArchive uses this access to create and organize your local archive. You can disable Notification Access at any time in Android Settings.

## 3. Local storage

Archived message content, archive metadata, generated snapshots and retained attachment files are designed to be stored locally on your device. ChatArchive does not require access to your WhatsApp account or WhatsApp chat database.

## 4. Exports and sharing

ChatArchive provides user-initiated features for opening, saving, sharing, exporting and backing up archived information. Information leaves the app's private storage only when you choose an export, save, share or backup action, or when required for a service you explicitly use.

## 5. Google Play Billing

Purchases of ChatArchive Pro are processed by Google Play. Google processes payment and purchase information under Google's own terms and privacy policies. ChatArchive receives purchase-status information needed to determine whether Pro features should be unlocked.

## 6. Security

ChatArchive includes local security features such as encrypted archive fields, encrypted backups, and optional PIN/biometric access controls. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection, so users should also protect access to their Android device and exported files.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Your local archive remains on your device until you delete records, clear the archive, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove the app's data. User-created exports and backups remain wherever you chose to save them until you delete them.

## 8. Children

ChatArchive is not specifically directed to children. Users should only archive communications where their use is lawful and appropriate.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when ChatArchive's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact **Elimara Technologies Limited** through the developer contact information published on ChatArchive's Google Play listing.
